## Standardized Landing Page Blueprint

*Every service, product, and main resource page will follow this strict section-by-section structural rule:*

### Section 1: Hero Section

* **Attention-Grabbing Headline:** Focused on the specific target audience (e.g., eco-lodges or wildlife operators).
* **Subheadline:** The core value proposition and specific outcome.
* **Primary CTA Button:** High-visibility action (e.g., "Book a Strategy Call", "Get a Free Audit").
* **Social Proof / Trust Badge:** Mini-testimonial or "As Seen In/Trusted By" logos of sustainable brands.

### Section 2: The Problem (Agitation)

* **Pain Point Bullet Points:** Highlighting common struggles specific to that niche (e.g., losing bookings to OTAs, poor search visibility for nature retreats).

### Section 3: The Solution (The Service/Product)

* **Core Offer Introduction:** How this specific service/product solves those exact pain points.
* **Key Features/Benefits Breakdown:** Grid layout connecting features to eco-tourism benefits.

### Section 4: Social Proof & Authority

* **Niche-Specific Case Study/Testimonial:** Deep dive into results achieved for a similar eco-tour client (metrics-focused, e.g., "+40% direct bookings").

### Section 5: Process / How It Works

* **3-4 Step Timeline:** Clear, friction-reducing steps on what happens after they click the CTA.

### Section 6: Custom FAQs

* **Dropdown Accordion:** 4–6 dynamic questions tackling objections *only* related to that specific page's service/product.

### Section 7: Final CTA (The Closer)

* **Urgency/Value Statement:** One final push reminding them of the transformation.
* **Primary CTA Button:** Replicated from the Hero section.

* **Custom FAQs:** Focused on session preparation, deliverables included, and follow-up support.
